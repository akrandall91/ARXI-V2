        By design

        Voice assistants

        No Alexa, Google Assistant or Siri inside. The mic measures loudness for the light and never listens for words or records.

        By design

        Cameras

        Presence comes from mmWave radar, which senses distance and movement but never captures images.

        Not yet

        HomeKit, Matter & SmartThings

        No direct support today. If you run Home Assistant, it can bridge many of these devices to your display.

        Not yet

        5 GHz-only Wi-Fi

        The displays need a 2.4 GHz network. Most routers broadcast both, so this rarely comes up.
