The room knows
the score.

A strip of full-color light for the wall above your bar or TV. It shows the time, the weather, tonight's games and the words to what's playing, then gets out of the way. No screen to stare at, no app to learn, no camera.

Full color

Any color, pixel by pixel.

A frosted diffuser softens every pixel into smooth, even light, so text reads cleanly from across the room. Each pixel takes its own color: team colors on scores, lyrics in each song's own color, icons in color, and light that moves with the room.

      Made for people who don't love gadgets

      Smart, without the smart-home homework

      Nothing to talk to

      There's no voice assistant and no camera. The built-in mic only measures how loud the room is, to drive the sound-reactive light. It doesn't listen for words or record anything.

      No app

      Change a setting from the browser on your phone while you're on home Wi-Fi.

      Quiet light

      A dim Night style and a red wind-down light keep it easy on the eyes after dark.
