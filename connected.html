    What Connected adds

    Display rolesRoles

    Each display gets a job. The Bar keeps the words: the time, the score, the lyrics. The Panel shows what goes with them: the weather when the Bar shows the temperature, the play when the Bar shows the score, pictures and visualizers when the Bar shows the lyrics.

    Two-way Gameday syncGameday

    Scores come from the Gameday dashboard, so the displays don't fetch them. The hub reports back, keeping the dashboard and the walls in step.

    Shared themesThemes

    One color theme across the house, or team colors everywhere on game day. Switch it once and every display follows.

    Local firstPrivacy

    The hub talks to your devices on your own network. It goes online only for public feeds like scores and weather. No camera, no voice assistant.

    How the hub worksGather · Decide · Draw · Report
    01 · Sources

    Gather

    The Gameday dashboard parses today's games in the browser. Sonos or HEOS, lyric timing, weather and room sensors report in.

    02 · Hub

    Decide

    The hub ranks what matters now. A score beats a lyric, a message beats a score, and every display gets the piece it needs.

    03 · Displays

    Draw

    Each display gets only what its role needs, so it spends its memory on drawing smooth text and light.

    04 · Dashboard

    Report

    The hub sends status back to the Gameday dashboard, so any screen in the house shows what every display is doing.

    Questions

    Before you connect

    How do my displays connect?

    Plug each display in and put it on your Wi-Fi. The hub finds every ARXI display on your network by itself and gives each one a job, with no pairing. Add a display later and it gets picked up the same way. Any order with two or more displays includes the hub. If you own one display, you can add the hub on its own for $49 and connect a second later, or add a second display and get the hub included.

    Can a Lite Bar join?

    Yes. Lite Bars take roles, alerts, themes and Gameday sync. Features that depend on presence sensing, like lighting up only the rooms in use, need a Pro Bar or Panel.

    What happens if the internet goes down?

    The hub keeps running on your home network. Clocks, room sensors and local music keep working. Scores and weather pick back up when the connection returns.

    What if the hub goes offline?

    Each display keeps showing its clock and last view until the hub is back, then catches up on its own.

    How does music work?

    ARXI reads what your Sonos or Denon HEOS speaker is playing, from any app, including Spotify and Apple Music. There are no music accounts to link. Without one of those speakers, everything else still works.

    How many displays can one hub run?

    The bundles cover two to four displays. Ask about larger homes in your quote.
